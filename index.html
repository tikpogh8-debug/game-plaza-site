GamePlaza

Five puzzle and arcade games in one app.

The games

  • Gleamfall: drag glittering crystal pieces onto the board and fill whole rows and columns to make them burst. Endless, with combos and full-board clears.
  • Bloomlight: turn mirrors, steer the light and wake every flower. 540 levels in ten gardens.
  • Skyline Stack: build the tallest tower you can, with four skylines to build in.
  • Unscrew Town: unscrew the planks in the right order, match the screws to their crates and never let the tray fill up. 720 levels.
  • Road Rush: an endless drive: switch lanes, dodge traffic, jump over cars and collect nitro through changing roads and weather.

Your progress stays on your phone: no account, no sign-in, nothing sent to a server. English and Russian.
