Privacy policy: GamePlaza

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Who is responsible: GamePlaza, contact: gameplazasupport@gmail.com

This policy explains what the game GamePlaza (Gleamfall, Bloomlight, Skyline Stack, Unscrew Town and Road Rush) stores, what is sent to others, why, and the choices you have.

In short

  • Your game progress and settings stay on your phone. We (GamePlaza) do not run a server and receive no data about you.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device data to show, measure and (if you allow it) personalise these ads.
  • In the EU, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before ads are personalised. On iPhone, the game asks for permission before tracking. You can change your choices at any time.

What the game stores on your phone

  • Your results (levels, stars, best scores, which tips you have seen) and your settings (sound, music, vibration, language, reduced motion, daily reminder).
  • This stays on your phone. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no server. Uninstalling the game deletes all of it.

Ads

The game shows ads in two ways:

  • Optional videos: you can choose to watch a short video for a reward, for example to continue a game after losing, to get extra moves, or to open an extra place in a level. Rewards from videos never cost you stars.
  • Short ads between plays: now and then, a short ad plays between levels or games. It can be closed after a few seconds.

The ads come from Google AdMob, provided by Google Ireland Limited (for users in the EU, the UK and Switzerland) and Google LLC (everywhere else). To show ads, the AdMob software in the game may collect and send to Google:

  • the device advertising identifier (Android advertising ID or Apple IDFA, only if you allow tracking on iPhone);
  • your IP address and the approximate location that comes from it (country or city, not your exact location);
  • device and app information (phone model, operating system, language, app version);
  • information about the ads shown and your interactions with them (views, taps, video completions);
  • diagnostic data about the ad software (for example crashes).

Google uses this data to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ads, prevent fraud and abuse, and, if you allow it, show ads based on your interests (personalised ads). We do not receive this data. How Google uses it: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Your choices about ads

  • EU, UK and Switzerland: when you first open the game, Google's consent form asks whether you agree to personalised ads. You can choose Consent, Do not consent, or Manage options. If you do not consent, you still see ads, but they are not personalised. To change your choice later, open More settings and tap Privacy choices.
  • iPhone: the game asks whether it may track you. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, your IDFA is not shared. You can change this in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: you can delete or reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads). Without it, ads are not personalised with it.
  • United States (for example California, Virginia, Colorado): showing personalised ads may count as "selling" or "sharing" personal data under some state laws. We do not sell your data for money. To opt out of personalised ads, use your phone's settings above (turn tracking off on iPhone, or delete the advertising ID on Android), or write to gameplazasupport@gmail.com.

Legal bases (EU, UK, Switzerland)

  • Personalised ads and storing or reading information on your device for them: your consent. You can withdraw it at any time (see above); this does not affect what happened before.
  • Non-personalised ads, ad measurement, and preventing fraud and abuse: our legitimate interest in funding a free game, and Google's in keeping ads safe.

International transfers

Google may process ad data outside your country, including in the United States. Google uses safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

How long data is kept

  • Data on your phone: until you delete it by uninstalling the game.
  • Ad data held by Google: as described in Google's privacy policy. We hold no data about you.

Analytics

The game has no analytics today. If we add any, this policy will say so first.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the game schedules a notification on your phone. It is stored on the phone only.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK, or California law), you have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to its use, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Since we hold no data about you, most requests concern Google's ad data: you can manage it with the choices above and at https://myadcenter.google.com. Write to gameplazasupport@gmail.com with any question; we answer within 30 days.

Children

The game is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game, write to us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and, for important changes, the game tells you.
