Privacy policy: GamePlaza

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Who is responsible: Playground Games, contact: gameplazasupport@gmail.com

This policy says what the game GamePlaza (Gleamfall, Bloomlight, Skyline Stack, Unscrew Town and Road Rush) stores, what it sends, and how you can delete it.

What the game stores on your phone

  • Your results (levels, stars, best scores, which tips you have seen) and your settings (sound, music, vibration, language, reduced motion, daily reminder).
  • Everything stays on your phone. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no server. Nothing you do is sent to us.
  • Uninstalling the game deletes all of it.

Ads

The game shows ads from Google AdMob, an advertising service, in two ways:

  • Optional videos: you can choose to watch a short video for a reward, for example to continue a game after losing, to get extra moves, or to open an extra place in a level. Rewards from videos never cost you stars.
  • Short ads between plays: now and then, a short ad plays between levels or games (for example after a few wins, or before a new game). It can be closed after a few seconds.

To show ads, AdMob may read a device advertising identifier and information about the ads you saw. We do not receive that information. You can reset or limit the advertising identifier in your phone's settings.

We do not sell your data, do not show personalised ads from our own data, and do not track you across other apps or websites. The game has no analytics today.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the game schedules a notification on your phone. It is stored on the phone only.

Your rights

You can see, change or delete everything in the game itself. Deleting it means uninstalling the game. Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR, or California), you can also ask us about your data. Since we hold none, there is nothing to send you; write to gameplazasupport@gmail.com if you have a question.

Children

The game is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and, for important changes, the game tells you.
